# Training Programs

Whether you're picking up gloves for the first time or preparing for trials, GKC has a program designed for you. Every session is built around specialised goalkeeper coaching — no outfield drills, no standing around waiting for your turn.

---

## Weekend Training Sessions

<span class="badge badge-active">Active</span>

Our core program: specialised goalkeeper sessions held every Saturday in Johannesburg. Small groups, individual attention, and progressive challenge.

### What a session covers

1. **Warm-up & activation** — Goalkeeper-specific movement, footwork, and body positioning
2. **Technical drills** — Shot-stopping, diving technique, catching, parrying, distribution
3. **Tactical work** — Positioning for crosses and corners, 1v1 scenarios, communication
4. **Game-realistic scenarios** — Simulated match situations that test decision-making under pressure
5. **Cool-down & debrief** — Review, individual feedback, and goal-setting

### Who it's for

- **Under 12s (ages 8–12)** — Building the foundations of goalkeeping
- **Juniors (ages 13–17)** — Developing technique and game understanding
- **Seniors (ages 18+)** — Refining skills and maintaining match sharpness

### Details

- **When**: Saturday mornings
- **Where**: Johannesburg (venue details provided on signup)
- **Duration**: 1.5–2 hours
- **Group size**: Small groups for maximum attention
