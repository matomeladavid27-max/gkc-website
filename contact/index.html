What to Include in Your Message

To help Coach Banele get back to you quickly, include:

  1. Your name (or your child's name if you're a parent)
  2. Age of the goalkeeper
  3. Current level — beginner, school team, club, etc.
  4. Which program you're interested in
  5. Any questions you have

Location

GKC's weekend training sessions are held in Johannesburg, Gauteng. Exact venue details are shared when you sign up for sessions.

For Parents

If you're a parent looking into goalkeeper training for your child, we understand you want to know it's safe, well-organised, and worth the investment. We welcome you to come watch a session before committing — just reach out and we'll arrange it.
